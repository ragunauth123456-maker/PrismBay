# Global Opportunity Engine

## Objective
Maintain a continuously refreshed queue of ecommerce opportunities and turn the best evidence-backed candidates into profitable, measurable sale experiments.

## Operating loop
1. Detect demand signals.
2. Corroborate demand and competition.
3. Screen for safety, logistics and return risk.
4. Source exact suppliers and variants.
5. Verify landed unit economics.
6. Build a rights-safe offer and channel plan.
7. Launch only after commercial gates pass.
8. Scale, revise or kill from verified order economics.

## Current commercialization market
US first because PrismBay Clean's existing Stripe and supplier freight controls are US-oriented. Discovery remains global and may recommend other markets only after payment, tax, shipping and platform requirements are verified.
