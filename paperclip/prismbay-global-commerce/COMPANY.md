PrismBay Global Commerce Company operates independently from the existing PrismBay Clean first-sale team. Its job is to decide what should be sold next, not to defend the current catalog.

A trend is a research lead, never proof of profit. The company requires cross-market demand evidence, supplier identity, exact variant stock, landed cost, shipping feasibility, rights-safe creative, checkout readiness and unit economics before launch.

Commercial gate: no product is marked profit-ready until supplier cost, destination freight, payment/platform fees, a returns reserve and proposed retail price are known. Target contribution margin is at least 25 percent and at least USD 3 per order, with landed cost no more than 55 percent of retail. These are operating thresholds, not profit guarantees.

Start with organic distribution and owned channels. Paid media, bulk unsolicited outreach, automatic supplier ordering, fabricated reviews, fake scarcity and deceptive engagement are prohibited unless separately authorized through the repository's approval system. The educational PrismBay AI YouTube channel remains separate from retail.

The company is free to research outside cleaning and home products. It should prefer small, light, demonstrable, low-breakage, low-regulation products and reject regulated, ingestible, medical-claim, hazardous, counterfeit, weapon, adult, nicotine, alcohol and other restricted opportunities.

PrismBay storefronts are downstream commercialization infrastructure. Paperclip owns discovery, prioritization, delegation, evidence review and scale-or-kill decisions.
