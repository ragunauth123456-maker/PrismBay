# PrismBay No-Idle Until First Sale Contract

Owner directive, 2026-10-03.

Until at least one genuine external PrismBay Clean sale is verified under the repository's sale-evidence standard, the commerce organization must not enter an idle, waiting-only, blocked-only, or no-work state.

## Mandatory operating state before first verified sale

Every control cycle must:

1. Keep the First Sale War Room active.
2. Assign active authorized internal work to all ten specialist companies.
3. Keep one primary product, one next-best product, and a broader research pipeline moving in parallel.
4. Convert every blocker into at least one legitimate fallback task.
5. Continue independent work even when an owner-only dependency exists.
6. Refresh verified commercial evidence before changing the scoreboard.
7. Schedule another operating cycle unless a verified sale has been established.

"Waiting", "blocked", "manual action required", or a failed integration is never the whole-company state while other authorized work exists.

## Required active assignment by company

- Product Intelligence: continuously search, compare, score, reject, and advance product candidates.
- Supplier and Fulfillment Intelligence: close the primary product's supplier/freight/economics gates while verifying backups in parallel.
- Offer Engineering: prepare evidence-backed pricing, bundles, upsells, cross-sells, and offer structures for the strongest verified or near-verified candidates without making unsupported live claims.
- Storefront Conversion: continuously audit and preflight page, disclosure, mobile, speed, checkout, confirmation, and order-capture readiness without releasing blocked products.
- Creative Studio: prepare truthful original or rights-cleared creative drafts for likely-to-clear products; do not publish blocked-product purchase claims.
- Organic Growth: research and prepare zero-cost distribution paths, search-intent opportunities, and channel plans that can activate once products are promotion-ready.
- Creator and Affiliate Growth: continuously research and qualify relevant creators and affiliate structures; do not send outreach without owner authorization.
- Revenue Analytics: refresh real-order evidence, detect stale metrics, maintain attribution readiness, and identify the next measurable funnel constraint.
- Experiment Lab: maintain and rank a low-cost experiment backlog with hypotheses, metrics, stop conditions, and next actions; do not label unmeasured work successful.
- Commerce Control: run the War Room, prevent duplicate work, reallocate workers to the bottleneck, enforce fallback routes, and keep the next operating cycle scheduled.

## Stop condition

The no-idle contract ends only when `verifiedSales >= 1` based on credible external payment evidence and the repository's required supplier, shipment, refund, and cost evidence.

After the first verified sale, control shifts to measurement, repeatability, margin improvement, fulfillment quality, and scaling only where evidence supports it.

All existing security, platform, KYC, payment, supplier, legal, customer-consent, owner-approval, media-rights, freight, checkout, and truthful-marketing controls remain authoritative. This contract requires persistence, not bypassing controls.
