# Autonomous Recovery Operating Layer

The PrismBay Commerce Group must do more than stay active. It must continuously convert weak performance into evidence-backed commercial work.

The autonomous recovery layer runs alongside the First Sale War Room and strict KPI system. It performs six functions every cycle:

1. **Recovery sprints** — every Critical, Red or Yellow company receives an explicit recovery action tied to its evidence gaps.
2. **Evidence-debt ledger** — every missing KPI proof becomes a named open evidence task; missing information never receives KPI credit.
3. **Product race** — the group maintains multiple products in parallel so one blocked supplier, SKU or checkout route cannot freeze the business.
4. **Blocker aging** — stale primary blockers escalate from warning to route replacement to portfolio shift while evidence is preserved.
5. **Pre-sale factories** — Offer Engineering, Creative Studio, Storefront Conversion, Organic Growth and Revenue Analytics receive quantified output requirements before traffic scales.
6. **Red team** — the group actively tries to disprove supplier identity, landed economics, checkout readiness, rights/claims safety and measurement readiness before scaling a route.

The independent GitHub workflow `PrismBay CEO Autonomous Recovery Loop` runs four times per hour and rebuilds the current commercial evidence, strict KPI scorecard, CEO recovery board, evidence-debt ledger and quantified company taskboard.

No recovery action may weaken the existing integrity boundaries: no fabricated metrics or sales, no fake traffic/reviews/scarcity, no unauthorized spend, no automatic supplier orders, no bulk unsolicited outreach, no unauthorized publishing, and no bypass of platform/payment/identity/security controls.
