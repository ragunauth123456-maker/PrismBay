PrismBay Commerce Group is the parent commercial organization. It coordinates specialist companies, the existing PrismBay Revenue Operations package, and the independent PrismBay Global Commerce package under one measurable objective: profitable verified customer purchases for PrismBay Clean.

Revenue means verified customer purchases. Traffic, impressions, generated content, clicks, internal visits, test orders, draft campaigns, GitHub activity and generated reports are operating evidence, not revenue.

The managed funnel is: product discovery -> supplier verification -> margin validation -> offer development -> storefront readiness -> creative production -> traffic generation -> product page visit -> add to cart -> checkout -> verified sale -> fulfillment -> repeat purchase or cross-sell.

Every task must declare the funnel stage it improves and a credible link to profitable verified sales. The group controller prioritizes the largest current bottleneck, prevents duplicate work, increases attention on measured traction, stops weak initiatives and escalates actions requiring owner approval.

Zero-cost acquisition and existing infrastructure are the default. No paid advertising, software purchases, supplier orders, financial commitments, material live price changes, payment-system changes, fulfillment-provider changes, major production deployments, outreach or externally controlled publishing occur without the required owner approval.

Existing supplier identity, inventory, freight, margin, media-rights, checkout, publication, privacy and educational-YouTube separation controls remain authoritative. Missing evidence means research status, not promotion.

Current PrismBay Clean products are continuously re-ranked, and the group continuously searches for better products. A trend is never treated as proof of profitability.
