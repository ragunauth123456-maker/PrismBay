# Continuous Growth Execution Layer

PrismBay does not treat a healthy workflow as commercial success. Until a verified profitable sale exists, the operating system must keep improving the probability and speed of a legitimate sale.

## New execution mechanisms

1. **Fair supplier race** — CJ sourcing keeps the top two candidates in every batch and rotates the remaining slots so lower-ranked products cannot starve indefinitely.
2. **Hourly supplier coverage** — the read-only supplier race runs hourly, subject to normal provider/API limits and fail-closed safeguards.
3. **Economics stress test** — every priced pack receives provisional retail-floor calculations under multiple fee scenarios. These are screening floors only, never market prices or launch approval.
4. **Supplier-evidence re-ranking** — research score is re-ranked using verified supplier identity, variant stock, screening freight, target-market origin and inventory.
5. **Pre-launch commercialization factory** — current candidates receive draft-only SEO, FAQ, objection, offer, creative, channel and measurement packages before launch gates clear.
6. **Conversion evidence contract** — worker requests, health checks and test payments never count as customer traffic or purchases.
7. **Commerce surface audit** — the store watchdog checks product feed structure, merchant feed items, sitemap URLs and robots directives in addition to HTTP uptime.

## Commercial boundaries

These mechanisms do not authorize paid spend, automatic supplier orders, bulk unsolicited outreach, unauthorized publishing, autonomous price changes, fake traffic, fake reviews, fake scarcity, fabricated sales, or bypassing platform/payment/identity/security requirements.

## Decision rule

A product or route earns more operating attention when it creates stronger verified evidence toward a real customer purchase. Trend popularity by itself cannot outrank better supplier/stock/freight evidence. Technical feed readiness never proves external marketplace or merchant-account approval.
