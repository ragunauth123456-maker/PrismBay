PrismBay Revenue Operations coordinates the existing PrismBay cloud workers under one goal hierarchy.

Revenue means a verified non-test paid order with supplier-order evidence, shipment evidence, no refund and complete cost evidence. Traffic, generated content, workflow success and storefront availability do not count as revenue.

The educational PrismBay AI YouTube channel stays separate from PrismBay Clean retail work.

Default operating rule: use zero-cost channels and existing cloud infrastructure. Do not incur spend, place supplier orders, enable checkout, send outreach, or publish retail content unless the repository's existing approval and evidence gates permit the action.
